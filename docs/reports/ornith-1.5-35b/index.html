16 GB · what actually worked

Only the IQ3 mix held 112k

Same 3060, -ngl 999, q8 KV, -c 131072. 16 GB means a 12 GB process cap (4 GB left for the OS).

IQ4_XS-IQ3_S · -ncmoe 22 16 GB full 8k–112k · ~56 → 31 t/s
Q4_K_M · q8 or q4 KV no q4 KV still died under the 16 GB cap
Q4_K-IQ4_XS · -ncmoe 23 no 16 GB ran to 64k, died at 112k
Q6_K 32 GB peak RSS ~22 G · -ncmoe 28

We did not quality-bench these GGUFs. Speeds are llama-benchy tg=256, 1 run, no warmup.
